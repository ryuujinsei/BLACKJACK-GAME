BLACKJACK

How to Play

  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.
  • Number cards are worth their number. J, Q and K are worth 10.
  • An Ace is worth 11, or 1 if 11 would take you over 21.
  • Click HIT to take another card. Click STAND to end your turn.
  • The dealer must keep drawing until reaching 17 or more.
  • If your score goes over 21, you lose.
